# Legal constraints (not legal advice)

Default implementation must not take custody of buyer merchandise funds.

Allowed: agents pay Clearlot for quotes/pool signals; buyers pay merchants; facilitator may hold our fee pending success.

Forbidden in this codebase: pooled USDC in an operator wallet; tradable lot tokens; collecting cards to charge later without a scoped Stripe SPT to a named merchant.

Maine licenses money transmitters with a large bond. $500 cannot buy that. Talk to a lawyer before holding anyone else’s money.
