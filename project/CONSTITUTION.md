# Constitution

Clearlot records specified industrial buy-intents, sells a live quote, and hands the buyer a merchant checkout URL. It does not sell parts, hold parts, or hold merchandise money.

Owner: HardwareHQ / @HardwareHQio. Not OC Detailing. Not a bank.

Forbidden: inventory; custody of buyer funds or cards; pooled escrow of customer USDC for goods; tokens/NFTs on lots; promising a fill; scraping merchant admin APIs; calling seed volume demand; claiming source-of-truth before fill is measured; one site per industry; vibe verticals.

Allowed: charge for quotes and later demand signals; buyer pays merchant; x402 fee to published payTo for our service only; one MCP / one payTo / one registry name.

An intent is a claim. A free search may be stale. A paid quote is true only if checkout could have filled.
